DefiMemory

The memory game with 6 levels and a multiplayer mode: memorize the cards, then find all the pairs.

▶ Play in your browser

How to play

At the start of each game, all the cards are shown for a few seconds, then they flip over. Tap two cards: if they match, they stay face up. Find all the pairs in as few moves as possible. Win a level to unlock the next one.

Multiplayer: 2 to 4 players on the same phone. Take turns flipping two cards. A pair is worth 1 point and you play again; otherwise, pass the phone to the next player. In a fair match, each player goes first in one round and the total number of pairs decides the winner (if tied: the most rounds won).

FAQ

I can't hear any sound. Check that the speaker icon is not crossed out, that the volume is up and, on iPhone, that the silent switch is off.

How do I unlock the Master level? Win the Expert level (15 pairs in under 2:30).

And the Legend level? Win the Master level. Watch out for look-alikes: 🍎 and 🍏 are not a pair!

My progress is gone. It is stored on the phone only, so it is deleted if the game is uninstalled.

The game is in the wrong language. DefiMemory follows your phone's language: French if your phone is set to French, English otherwise.
