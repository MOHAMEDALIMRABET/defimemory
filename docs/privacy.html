DefiMemory Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

DefiMemory is a memory card game. This page explains what data the game uses. In short: the game does not collect any personal data; only online play with a friend sends a nickname and an anonymous identifier, for the duration of the game.

Data collected

Apart from online play, none. DefiMemory does not ask for an account, a name or an email address, and it does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

The game only stores, on your phone, your best scores, the unlocked levels, the player names you type in multiplayer mode and your sound setting. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

Online play with friends

If you use the "Online" mode, the game connects to Google Firebase (servers in Belgium, European Union). It only stores: an anonymous identifier created automatically (no name, email or phone number), the nickname you choose, the room code and the cards flipped during the game. This information is only used to let both players play together; your opponent can see your nickname and score, and it is deleted when the players leave the room. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Weekly league

For the weekly ranking, after each Online game the game stores on Google Firebase your online nickname, your points, pairs found, wins and number of games for the week, the code of your last room and the anonymous identifier. Solo and Multiplayer games are not sent. This nickname and these results are visible to other players in the ranking. To have this data deleted, write to the contact address below.

Advertising, analytics and sharing

DefiMemory shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools and shares no data with third parties, apart from hosting online games on Google Firebase. Solo and Multiplayer modes work without an Internet connection.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data, it collects no data about children either.
