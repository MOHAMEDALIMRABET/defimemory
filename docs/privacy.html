DefiMemory Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

DefiMemory is a memory card game. This page explains what data the game uses. In short: the game does not collect any personal data.

Data collected

None. DefiMemory does not ask for an account, a name or an email address, and it does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

The game only stores, on your phone, your best scores, the unlocked levels, the player names you type in multiplayer mode and your sound setting. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

Advertising, analytics and sharing

DefiMemory shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools and shares no data with third parties. The game works without an Internet connection.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data, it collects no data about children either.
